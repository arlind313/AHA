### Style lock
> Premium cinematic graphic-novel illustration, detailed hand-drawn comic look, strong clean black ink
> outlines of varying weight, cel-shaded colour with soft painted gradients, realistic body proportions,
> professional cinematic lighting, matching the line quality, shading and colour treatment of the attached
> character sheet. Single full-bleed vertical frame, 9:16, 1080×1920 or larger.

### Colour grade lock (Ireland, 2017)
> Cool, slightly desaturated Irish daylight: soft grey-blue sky light, muted greens and slate greys,
> warm tungsten/sodium accents only from interior practicals. Gentle contrast, no teal-orange blockbuster grade,
> no sunny palm-tree tones from the reference sheet's backgrounds.

### Character lock – YOUNG CONOR (2017, age 16–17)
Derived only from the attached comic sheet, de-aged:
- Same face shape: strong straight brows, deep-set eyes, straight nose, defined jaw and chin, slightly
  prominent ears — softer, fuller cheeks and smoother skin than the adult version.
- Hair: short dark brown, faded/short sides, slightly longer textured top pushed up and forward (same
  silhouette as the reference, a touch less groomed).
- Facial hair: none or faint teenage fuzz on upper lip/chin — **no full beard**.
- **No tattoos anywhere** (arms, hands, neck all clear). No watch, no jewellery.
- Build: lean, wiry teenager, natural proportions — not the adult's muscular frame.
- Wardrobe (identical in all 3 previews): faded navy work trousers with knee patches, plain dark-grey
  long-sleeve top / work jumper with sleeves pushed up, scuffed black safety boots, grubby hands.
  Preview 02 may drop the grime but keep the same clothes.

### Car lock – Honda Civic (7th gen, 2001–2005 European 3-door hatchback)
> Early-2000s Honda Civic 3-door hatchback (UK-built EP-series), tall one-box "bullet" profile, short sloping
> bonnet flowing straight into a steeply raked windscreen, large swept-back teardrop headlights, small narrow
> grille, three doors, tall upright rear hatch with vertical tail lights, plain body-colour bumpers,
> standard 15-inch wheels with plain silver covers, factory ride height. Colour: **satin silver**
> (keep identical in 02 and 03). Number plates blank / out of focus, no badges legible.

> Note for accuracy: in European spec, the **1.4 petrol** 7th-gen hatch is the **EP1**; **EP2** is the 1.6 and
> **EP3** is the Type R. All three share the same 3-door body shell, so the visuals are identical — but please
> confirm which code you want used in the narration.
